# Av. Asistan

**AI-powered legal practice management for Turkish lawyers.**

🌐 Website: https://www.avasistan.com.tr

Av. Asistan is a cloud-based practice management platform built for the Turkish legal system. It brings cases, clients and documents together in one workspace and automatically imports data from UYAP and UETS via a browser extension. Hearings and legal deadlines appear on a shared calendar with smart reminders, and everything syncs in real time across web, iOS and Android.

## Features

- AI Assistant and Petition Studio (AI drafting, document summaries, smart OCR)
- UYAP and UETS integration
- Case, client and document management
- Hearing and deadline calendar with smart reminders, Google Calendar sync
- Precedent search: Yargıtay, Danıştay, Constitutional Court
- Financial ledger per case and client
- Team roles and task assignment (lawyer, intern, secretary)
- Web, iOS, Android and Chrome extension
- KVKK compliance, role-based access, encrypted storage

## Who is it for?

Solo lawyers, law firms of every size, in-house counsel and trainee lawyers.
